# Editorial contract

This Week in Crypto is an English Thursday digest of crypto news, projects and research. The public MIT repository owns assets and published archives; the private generator owns discovery and editorial working material.

Use a complete seven-day Asia/Shanghai window ending Thursday at 00:00. Keep event, source publication and discovery dates separate. A proposed upgrade is not a deployed upgrade, and a newly discovered project is not necessarily newly launched.

Read original material before writing factual claims. Distinguish issuer claims, independent reporting and community opinion. For financial data, retain units, measurement periods, rights, custody and redemption conditions when material; TVL, balances, volume, net inflows, usage and revenue are different quantities.

Use the generator's editable sections and sole Formatted template. Each reader item is one concise descriptive link with essential qualifications in its linked text. Give each event one primary placement, including Picks, and remove unresolved placeholders and empty sections before publication.

Review source coverage and evidence alongside the draft. No generated edition is automatically published, copied here or archived. Only archive an issue after its actual publication is confirmed.
