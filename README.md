# This Week in Crypto

![This Week in Crypto — a weekly selection of crypto news, projects, and research](assets/banner.png)

**This Week in Crypto** is a weekly newsletter published every Thursday, bringing together crypto news, projects, and research worth a closer look.

Each issue includes concise introductions and links to original sources, helping you understand what changed and choose what to explore further.

[Subscribe on Substack](https://thatweekincrypto.substack.com/) to receive the latest issue in your inbox.

## Suggest content

Found a useful project, research report, governance discussion, or important development?

[Submit an issue](https://github.com/z08-studio/this-week-in-crypto/issues/new?template=content-suggestion.yml) to suggest it for a future edition.
